ENGINEERING CONSTRAINT

File availability depended on other users being online, and home Internet connections were often slow. A single MP3 could take many minutes to transfer over dial-up.

MAIN LESSON

Napster demonstrated how centralized discovery and distributed users could work together. The architecture helped popularize peer-to-peer networking while changing how people thought about distributing digital media.
